## 3–5 sentence
Grafe Auction is a commercial and industrial auction and liquidation company founded in 1959.
It conducts online auctions for commercial assets, industrial equipment, and real estate,
serving buyers and sellers across the United States. Sellers use Grafe to move equipment,
fixtures, inventory, and real property during business closures, relocations, restructurings,
and lender or court-supervised dispositions. Buyers browse active listings and bid online
under the terms published on each auction. For current listings, timelines, and official
policies, refer to grafeauction.com.
